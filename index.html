🤖 Lesson 1: What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is technology that helps computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples of AI:

  • Chatbots that answer questions.
  • Maps that suggest routes.
  • Tools that help write, translate or create images.

Remember: AI can make mistakes. Check important information before trusting it.

✨ Lesson 2: How to Use AI

You can give an AI tool an instruction or question. This instruction is called a prompt.

Example of a weak prompt: "Teach me."

Example of a better prompt: "Explain HTML in simple English with one example for a beginner."

Try these steps:

  1. Tell the AI what you want to do.
  2. Explain your level or goal.
  3. Ask for a clear example.
  4. Ask follow-up questions if you need help.

Safety tip: Do not share passwords, private information or verification codes with AI tools.
